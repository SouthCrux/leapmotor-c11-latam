# Regional scope / Alcance regional

**Goal, not an existing coverage claim.** RC1 is based on one pilot platform in Uruguay. Even there, owner-confirmed map behavior does not establish complete national map coverage, address quality or every driving scenario.

**Objetivo, no cobertura ya conseguida.** La meta abarca todos los países latinoamericanos. Para evitar exclusiones, también se contemplan Belice, Guyana y Surinam, y la participación de Puerto Rico y otras comunidades/territorios de la región. No se presupone que todos compartan datos, idioma o firmware.

Puerto Rico and other regional territories/communities are welcome as additional validation areas; this does not classify them as sovereign countries. Their status is also unvalidated.

Each regional port needs suitable map/data permissions, coordinate handling, address/search validation, routing tests, both-display behavior and language review. Spanish, Portuguese, French, Haitian Creole, English, Dutch and locally requested languages should be considered according to actual community needs. No translation into all these languages is claimed today.
